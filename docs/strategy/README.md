## Fixed by the PRD

- **Positioning (§4):** PXTO connects systems, automates processes and builds
  digital solutions. It is **not** defined by any single label — not an agency,
  not a website developer, not a consultancy, not an AI company, not a low-code
  automation company, not a traditional software house. Each is part of the
  capability; none defines the brand.
- **Value proposition (§5):** PXTO turns real business needs into digital
  solutions by connecting systems, automating processes and developing bespoke
  software. Technology is the means, business results the end.
- **Business objective (§3):** the site is primarily a commercial opportunity
  generation tool. A visitor must quickly answer: what PXTO is, what it does,
  what problems it solves, whether it could build what they need, and how to
  make contact.
- **Audience (§8):** business owners, partners, directors, operations /
  commercial / administrative managers, process owners; companies running
  multiple systems, companies with manual processes, companies needing a
  specific solution built.
- **Metrics (§51):** business (leads, contact conversion rate, projects
  originated by the site), behaviour (CTA CTR, project views, interaction time,
  scroll depth), technical (Core Web Vitals, performance, errors, uptime).
  **No numeric targets are to be set before sufficient history exists.**
